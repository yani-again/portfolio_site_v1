About Me

I'm Yanislav Yankov, a 2nd year BSc Computer Science student at the University of Hertfordshire 🇬🇧. During my 1st year, I discovered a deep interest in embedded systems and low-level coding (especially in the C language).

Right now I'm working with an STM32H7, learning it the way I tend to learn new things: by building everything from scratch so I can truly understand everything.

I use Linux daily, enjoy working with microcontrollers (RP2040, STM32H7), and care about understanding how things work without any abstractions - hence the quote above this paragraph.

You can also find my blog below, I share everything I learn as I build towards an embedded software role. Join me as I figure this out!
